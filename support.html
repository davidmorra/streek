Common questions

Will a day off break my streak?
No. Days your schedule doesn't include never break a streak.

Can I check in for yesterday?
Yes. Long-press a habit and use the Yesterday menu.

If I change a habit's goal or schedule, do I lose my history?
No. Past days and streaks keep the plan they were tracked with.

Is my data synced or backed up?
Your habits are stored only on this iPhone. They are included in your normal iCloud or computer backup of the device, but there is no sync between devices yet.

How do I delete my data?
Delete a habit from its menu, or delete the app to remove everything.
