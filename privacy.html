Streek Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Streek is a habit tracker for iPhone made by Daviti Keropiani. This page explains what the app does with your data.

Your habits stay on your iPhone

Everything you enter in Streek (habit names, notes, icons, colors, check-ins and history) is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. There is no account and no sign-up. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Your own iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone may include it, as with any app.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features people use, Streek sends anonymous usage statistics through TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany. These include events such as "app opened", "habit created" or "check-in", the kind of habit (for example "count" or "every day"), how many habits you have, your sort and layout choice, and basic technical details such as app version, iOS version and device model.

They never include habit names, notes or anything else you type. TelemetryDeck receives a hashed, anonymous identifier instead of your name, email, Apple ID or IP address, so the data can't be used to identify you. It is not used for advertising and not sold or shared with data brokers. See TelemetryDeck's privacy policy.

Tracking and ads

Streek does not track you across other apps or websites and does not show ads.

Children

Streek does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
